+ I like to build things [read: LEGO sets, nothing related to software, lol]. There are times I create problems just to solve them [oooh, classic red flag].
+ Just thought I’d write something on the internet—it's mostly me talking to myself.
+ I like to think of myself as a modern-day explorer… um, sometimes.
+ I believe the best way to solve any problem (or avoid one) is by watching 9–10 seasons of sitcoms or reading fiction books (eh, mostly series).
+ Random info: I thought wearing specs was cool until I started wearing them (no choice). It sucks.
+ The more I try to "live life" by doing some curveball stuff, the more it just doesn’t work out. Slowly realizing that "living life" is just hanging out with people you like at that restaurant and talking for hours, watching that movie, moving my body, going for long walks, jumping into rabbit holes as soon as I see them, not having opinions on everything, and avoiding drama at all costs. That’s it. It’s that simple. But eh, who am I to say? I’ve just completed a couple of decades on planet Earth.
